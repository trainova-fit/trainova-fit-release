Before your first session

Set up your phone so it can see you

Feedback is only as good as what the camera sees. Spend a minute on this before your first set.

  • Whole body in frameHead to feet, with a little space above and below. If your feet are cut off, move the phone back.
  • Phone upright and steadyProp it in portrait mode at about waist height, around 2 to 3 metres away. A water bottle or bench works if you don't have a stand.
  • Light in front of youAvoid standing in front of a bright window. Backlight hides your joints.
  • Keep the space in front of you clearPeople walking or standing between you and your phone can block the camera and interrupt the tracking.
  • Sound onNovi gives voice cues during the set, so turn up your volume or use earphones.

Privacy and permissions

What the app sees and keeps

Camera

The camera tracks your joints while you train. Video is processed on your phone. Trainova Fit doesn't record or upload your video.

Your workout data

Your sets, reps, form scores, weights and RPE are saved so you can review your progress over time.

Permissions

Camera is the only permission Trainova Fit asks for. Voice coaching plays through your speaker, so the app doesn't use your microphone.

Questions

Common questions

How do I update the app?

The app doesn't update itself. Come back to this page, download the latest APK, and install it over the old one. Your saved workouts stay, as long as you don't uninstall first. Check all releases to see what changed.

Is the download safe?

The APK is published on GitHub by the Trainova Fit team, and each release lists its SHA-256 checksum so you can check the file wasn't changed. The warnings you see come from Android, which shows them for every app installed outside the Play Store.

Does it work on iPhone?

No. Trainova Fit is Android only for now.

Do I need a wearable or extra equipment?

No. All you need is your phone and something to prop it up with, plus the weights for your workout.

Is it a replacement for a coach?

No. Trainova Fit gives exercise guidance on five exercises. It isn't medical advice. If something hurts, stop, and follow your gym's safety rules.

I found a bug. Where do I report it?

Use Send feedback in the app to report it. If the app won't open, email trainovafitsupport@gmail.com instead. Either way, include your phone model, Android version and what you were doing when it happened. A screenshot helps a lot.
