A set, from first rep to summary

Prop your phone up, do your set, then read the summary. This sample set shows what the app screens look like.

  • The camera tracks 33 points on your body, right on your phone.
  • Each rep is counted, and the coach calls out the number.
  • A named mistake is flagged once per rep, and the coach says it out loud.
  • When the set ends you get a summary.
  • See which reps were clean across all three sets.
  • Log how hard the set felt.

          Install in five steps

          Android will ask you to confirm a few times. This is normal for any app you download from a website. It takes about a minute.

          1. 1

            Download the APK

            Tap Download APK at the top of this page. Chrome may warn that the file might be harmful. Chrome shows this for every APK file, so tap Download anyway.

          2. 2

            Open the file

            When the download finishes, tap Open on the pop-up or the notification. If you miss it, open your Files app and look in Downloads.

          3. 3

            Allow installs from your browser

            The first time, Android asks you to allow apps from this source. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.

            You only do this once. You can turn it off again after installing.

          4. 4

            Tap Install

            If Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway. It warns about apps that aren't from the Play Store. It doesn't mean the app is unsafe.

            Buttons have slightly different names on Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo and Vivo phones. The steps are the same.

          5. 5

            Open the app and start training

            Open Trainova Fit, explore the app, and start your first exercise. When asked, allow camera access and choose While using the app, so Trainova Fit can see your form.

            Future updates arrive inside the app, so you only need this page for the first install.

          Set up your phone so it can see you

          • Whole body in frameHead to feet, with a little space above and below. If your feet are cut off, move the phone back.
          • Phone upright and steadyProp it in portrait mode at about waist height, around 2 to 3 metres away. A water bottle or bench works if you don't have a stand.
          • Light in front of youAvoid standing in front of a bright window. Backlight hides your joints.
          • Keep the space in front of you clearPeople walking or standing between you and your phone can block the camera and interrupt the tracking.
          • Sound onTurn your volume up or wear earphones. Your coach speaks corrections out loud, and with your phone a few steps away, cues are easy to miss over gym music.

          What the app sees and keeps

          Camera and voice

          • Camera. Frames are processed on your phone. Video and images are never recorded, saved or uploaded.
          • Voice coaching. Pre-recorded clips come with the app, with your phone's text-to-speech as a fallback.
          • Microphone. Never used. The camera runs with audio off.
          • Permissions. The app asks for camera access. Install unknown apps is asked once, only so the app can install its own updates.

          Your data

          • Saved data. Your account, profile, workouts and sets (reps, weight, form score, flagged corrections, RPE), steps, streaks and XP. It is stored over encrypted connections. If you are offline, set writes wait on your phone until a connection returns.
          • Visible to others. Your display name and XP appear on the leaderboard. A Weekly Recap is shared with friends, and you can turn it off.

          Account and updates

          • Sign-in. Google, or email and password. Email sign-up sends a confirmation link, and a reset link if you need one. Before sign-up, only the part of your email after the @ is checked with Google Public DNS.
          • Update check. A plain request to GitHub at most every 6 hours, with no personal or workout data.
          • Age and internet. You must be 18 or older, and the app needs an internet connection.
          • No ads and no data selling. The app follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

          Common questions

          How do I update the app?

          Updates happen inside the app. Trainova Fit checks for a new version when you open it and shows an Update dialog with what changed. Tap Update, then Install when Android asks. You can also check yourself at Settings → System → Updates → Check for updates.

          You don't need to uninstall first, and your data stays. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from Trainova Fit. If an update ever fails, download the latest APK from this page and install it over the old one. All releases lists every change.

          Which Android versions work?

          Android 10 and above.

          Is the APK safe?

          The APK is published on GitHub by the Trainova Fit team and signed with the team's own key. Each release lists its SHA-256 checksum so you can check the file wasn't changed, and Android only accepts an update signed with that same key. The warnings you see come from Android, which shows them for every app installed outside the Play Store.

          Why does it need the camera?

          The camera tracks your joints while you train. Video is processed on your phone. Trainova Fit doesn't record or upload your video.

          What equipment do I need?

          All you need is your phone and something to prop it up with, plus the weights for your workout. There is no wearable to buy.

          Earphones help. Your coach speaks corrections out loud, and your phone is usually a few steps away, so cues are easy to miss over gym music. Trainova Fit doesn't need any extra permission for earphones.

          Wear fitted clothes. The camera follows your joints, so close-fitting pants and a fitted top help it see your knees, hips and elbows. Very baggy pants or loose tops can hide them and make tracking less steady.

          Does it replace a coach?

          No. Trainova Fit gives exercise guidance on five exercises. It isn't medical advice. If something hurts, stop, and follow your gym's safety rules.

          Something isn't working

          Check the Stuck? list in the install steps first. If that doesn't help, email trainovafitsupport@gmail.com with your phone model, Android version and a screenshot.

          How do I report a bug?

          In the app, open the Profile tab, then Help & Feedback, then Send feedback. It doesn't include your email, so the team can't reply to it. Email trainovafitsupport@gmail.com if you want a reply, and tell us your phone model, Android version and what you were doing.

          Do I need earphones?

          No, but they help. Your coach speaks corrections out loud, and your phone is usually a few steps away, so cues are easy to miss over gym music. Trainova Fit doesn't need any extra permission for earphones.

          Trainova Fit is an Android app that uses your phone camera to track your movement. It gives real-time form feedback and voice coaching for five gym exercises, and it builds a 30-day plan from your fitness level, equipment and the areas you want to avoid.
